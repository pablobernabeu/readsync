Interlexis: webcam reading demo

All processing happens in your browser. No video, gaze or calibration data leaves this device.

This demo estimates where you are looking from your webcam, taking the position of your head into account, entirely on your machine. It calibrates on nine dots shown at two head positions, then shows short sentences with a live gaze dot. It uses the gaze model of the readsync toolkit, ported to the browser.

Webcam gaze is much less precise than a laboratory eye-tracker, so treat the dot as a rough guide to where you are looking. Sit facing a light so that your face is evenly lit, keep your head fairly still and recalibrate when asked.
