Interlexis: webcam reading demo

All processing happens in your browser with your webcam. No video, gaze or calibration data leaves this device, and nothing is uploaded.

This is the browser benchmark strand of Interlexis, a project on reading in a second language: head-pose-aware webcam gaze, running entirely on your machine. It calibrates against a few on-screen dots, then shows short sentences with a live gaze dot. It runs the same gaze pipeline as the readsync desktop toolkit, ported to the browser.

Webcam gaze is coarse. It resolves a region of a line, not a single letter. Sit with the screen well lit, keep your head fairly still, and recalibrate when asked.
